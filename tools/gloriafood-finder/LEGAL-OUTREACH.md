# UK Outreach Guardrails — GloriaFood Migration Campaign

Practical guidance, **not legal advice**. For high-volume sending, get a quick compliance review.
Framework: UK GDPR + PECR (as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025).

## The one question that decides everything

**Is the restaurant a corporate subscriber (Ltd company / LLP) or an individual subscriber
(sole trader / ordinary partnership)?** Check on [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/) (free) *before* contacting.

| Channel | Ltd company / LLP | Sole trader / partnership |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold email** | ✅ Allowed without prior consent. Must identify Posso, give a reply/postal address, working opt-out, link your privacy policy. (Rely on legitimate interest for the named person's work address.) | ❌ Treated like a consumer — no cold email without consent or a qualifying soft opt-in. Use another channel. |
| **Cold call** | ✅ If number not on **CTPS** or your own do-not-call list | ✅ If number not on **TPS** or your own do-not-call list |
| **LinkedIn** | ✅ Outside PECR. Lowest regulatory risk. | ✅ Same |
| **Postal mail** | ✅ Not covered by PECR. Address to "The Manager". | ✅ Same |
| **In-person visit** | ✅ Highest-trust channel for local independents | ✅ Same |

## Non-negotiables

- **Screen every phone number against BOTH TPS and CTPS** (plus your own list) before calling.
  Re-screen at least every **28 days**; log the screening date per campaign. Present a valid caller ID.
- **Identify Posso + offer an opt-out** in every email and call.
- **Central suppression list** across all channels — anyone who opts out / says stop goes on it
  permanently and is checked before every campaign.
- Prefer **generic mailboxes** (`info@`, `bookings@` on the restaurant's own domain) over
  named-individual addresses — lowest GDPR/PECR risk.

## One-page compliance file to keep

1. A short **Legitimate Interests Assessment** (purpose: offering a GloriaFood replacement before
   the April 2027 shutdown; necessity; balancing test — B2B, directly relevant, low intrusion).
2. A **privacy notice** on posso.co.uk stating how you obtained business contacts and how to object;
   link it in every email.
3. The **suppression list** + **TPS/CTPS screening date records**.

## Golden rules

> Ltd/LLP = cold email OK. Sole trader/partnership = no cold email — phone (screened), LinkedIn,
> post, or visit. All phone = screen TPS+CTPS. Always identify yourself + offer opt-out.

The hook that strengthens your legitimate-interest case: GloriaFood is **forcing** these
businesses to migrate by 30 April 2027 — your outreach addresses a genuine, time-sensitive need.
